Learning across protein sequence, structure, and language.

I am a PhD student in Medical Bioinformatics at the School of Basic Medical Sciences, Peking University. My interests include multimodal protein representation learning, function retrieval, and the selective acquisition of structural information.

Explore the Project Research Interests GitHub

Research Interests

  1. Multimodal protein learning

    Connecting protein sequences, structural representations, and functional descriptions.

  2. Protein function retrieval

    Retrieving proteins from descriptions of molecular function and evaluating the resulting rankings.

  3. Selective structural information

    Studying when additional structural information is useful and how to prioritize its acquisition.

Featured Project

Protein Function Retrieval and Structure-Upgrade Gain Prediction

An analysis of 2,000 proteins and 28 CAFA5-derived function queries, examining when replacing a structural representation improves retrieval—and why identifying beneficial replacements does not necessarily maximize retrieval gain.
